FORGE

Field manual

Everything the app does, in the order you'll need it. Fifteen minutes to read once; after that, the screens explain themselves.

01

Setup

If FORGE is already on your home screen, skip to step 4.

  1. Open the FORGE link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right, then Add to Home screen and Install.
  3. Open FORGE from the new icon from now on. It runs full screen and works without signal in the gym.
  4. Go to Settings, enter your body weight, tap Save settings. The bodyweight exercises depend on it.
  5. Turn your phone's media volume up. The rest timer's beeps play through it, not the ringer.

Your workouts are stored on this phone, in Chrome. Nothing is sent anywhere. That makes backups your job; section 08 covers it, and the app will remind you.

02

The program

Six workouts in a fixed rotation. The A days are power: heavy weight, low reps, long rest. The B days are hypertrophy: moderate weight, more reps, controlled tempo. Strength builds on the A days, size and work capacity on the B days.

P1 · B1 · L1
Push A, Pull A, Legs A (power)
P2 · B2 · L2
Push B, Pull B, Legs B (hypertrophy)

Pull days use B, for back, because two P's would collide.

No fixed weekdays

Home always shows the next workout in the rotation, whenever you train. Take a day off and nothing shifts: tap ✕ Rest on Home if you want the calendar to show it. A rest day never costs you a workout.

RPE: how hard a set should feel

Each exercise lists an RPE target, a 1 to 10 effort scale counted in reps left in the tank.

RPE 7
Three more reps were there.
RPE 8
Two more reps were there.
RPE 9
One more rep, maybe.
RPE 10
Nothing left. The program rarely asks for this.

Pick the weight that lands you on target, not the weight you hope to lift. If the last set hits the target, go up next time.

Colors

Color only shows where something is happening: today, the current set, the timer.

Amber
Power days
Cyan
Hypertrophy days
Green
Calisthenics (no-gym) workouts
Violet
Back & Core
Grey

Swapping a workout

Tap SWAP on Home to pick a different session. The calisthenics versions need only a bar and a bench, for travel or a skipped gym day; by default they count as the day they replace, so the rotation moves on. Back & Core is a bonus session and doesn't move the rotation. The Counts as toggle overrides either.

03

Sizes

Every workout comes in three sizes. Each one is the smaller one plus more, so Light is always the core of the day.

− Light
About 30 minutes. The essentials.
✱ Normal
About 60 minutes. The standard day.
+ Extended
About 85 minutes. Everything.
Size locks at the start

The start button names the size, for example START NORMAL ✱. Read it before you tap. Once a workout starts, its size can't be changed.

04

A workout, start to finish

  1. On Home, tap START WORKOUT. Pick the size, then tap the start button.
  2. The overview lists the warm-up, then the exercises. A Primer is an explosive move right after the warm-up; a Finisher closes the session. Tap any line to go to it; the order is a suggestion.
  3. Do the warm-up, then tap Warm-up complete.
  4. On each exercise, set the weight with −5 +5 (or type it), set the reps with −1 +1, and tap Save set.
  5. The rest timer starts on its own. At three seconds left it beeps 3, 2, 1, then a high tone at zero; the phone also buzzes if the screen is on. +15s buys more rest, Stop ends it.
  6. After the last set, tap Next exercise. When every exercise is done, the overview shows Complete workout.

On the exercise screen

  • Last time is the heaviest set from your previous session. That's today's target. Best set is your record. Trend appears after three sessions.
  • Tips shows an animated figure of the movement with its form cues; tap the figure to pause it. Demo opens a video.
  • Plates is a plate calculator: pick the bar, tap plates to load one per side, and it fills in the weight.
  • Made a typo? Tap the set's pill (Set 2 ✓), fix it, tap Update set.
  • Skip moves on without logging. You can come back from the overview.
  • Timed holds (dead hangs, wall sits) show a stopwatch instead of reps. Tap it to start, tap again to stop; the seconds fill in.

Leaving mid-workout

Answer the phone, switch apps, lock the screen: nothing is lost. Home shows a RESUME card that returns you to the exact set. To quit early, tap End on the overview; any sets you logged are kept.

05

Bodyweight moves

Pull-ups, dips and similar moves have a four-way toggle. It changes what the weight box means.

BW
Bodyweight only. Just log reps.
BW+
Weight added with a belt or dumbbell. Enter only the added pounds. The box reads Added.
BW−
An assisted machine. Enter the assistance shown on the machine's stack. The box reads Assist.
Free
Plain weight, no bodyweight math.

FORGE remembers your choice for each exercise. Progress on BW− means the assistance number going down; the app ranks it that way.

When you go unassisted

Log it as BW+ with 0 added, not as plain BW. Switching to plain BW resets that exercise's record to a reps count and throws away your assisted history as a baseline.

06

Records

Beat your best set and FORGE celebrates: hammer, clang, sparks, and the margin over the old record. Tap to dismiss.

  • The first time you log an exercise, it becomes the record quietly. Celebrations start on the second session.
  • A record is the heaviest real set, not an estimate. More reps at the same weight also counts.
  • The PRs tab lists every record, grouped into weighted, bodyweight and timed.
07

Calendar and history

Home shows this week as a strip. Tracker has the full month and your last ten workouts; tap one for every set.

P1−
A light Push A. The mark after the code is the size: − light, ✱ normal, + extended.
✕
A rest day.
·
A past day with nothing logged.
P2
Faded codes on today and future days are what's coming up.

Tap any past day to correct it: mark rest, add a workout you did without the app, or delete an entry. The rotation recalculates from what the calendar says. Key on the Tracker tab explains every symbol.

08

Backups

Your history lives only on this phone. A lost, broken or reset phone takes it along unless there's a copy somewhere else.

  1. Settings → Back up data.
  2. The share menu opens. Send it to Drive, email it to yourself, or text it to Jonny.

The file is called something like forge-backup-2026-10-06.txt. The .txt is normal; Android won't share the other file type.

Once you have a workout more than a week old, Home shows an amber Back up now bar until you do. After that it comes back every two weeks. Settings shows when you last did it.

Restoring

New phone, or something went wrong: install FORGE (section 01), then Settings → Import data from JSON and pick the backup file. The .txt works.

The easy way to lose everything

Clearing Chrome's cookies and site data, often done to "speed up the phone", erases FORGE along with it. Clearing the cache alone is fine. Back up first if you ever do the full clear.

09

Sandbox

A practice mode on a throwaway copy of your data. Log sets, finish workouts, press every button. Nothing touches the real thing.

  • Enter: tap the FORGE logo, top left, five times quickly, then confirm.
  • You're in it while striped hazard tape runs under the header.
  • Exit: tap SANDBOX · EXIT at the top. Closing the app fully also ends it.

Worth five minutes before your first gym session.

10

Feedback

This copy is yours, and the program can bend to fit you. After a few sessions, text Jonny what isn't working. The useful kind is specific:

  • An exercise your gym can't do, or your joints don't like.
  • A session that runs long or short for its size.
  • Anything on screen that made you stop and guess.

Updates arrive by themselves. If a screen ever looks odd right after one, close FORGE fully and reopen it.
